Concept for the Qloo hackathon

Archetype: the cultural-fit brief for anything you are about to launch

Give it a book, game, film, show, podcast or album. The agent builds an evidence-backed brief on who the audience really is, which artists and partners they would accept as authentic, where and when to launch, and which generic suggestions an LLM alone would get wrong.

The idea in one paragraph

Who uses it

Mara, a brand strategist at a studio adapting a cult novel into a series. Today she gets brand and music suggestions from a deck of clichés and a small, slow focus group. A wrong partner means fans call the launch "inauthentic" on day one.

Same buyer type: marketing agencies, game and film publishers, author and music managers, brands planning a collaboration.

What she gets in 2 minutes

  1. Audience DNA: measured age and gender skew, taste tags, what else they love.
  2. Sound and talent: mainstream and emerging artists whose fans overlap, for trailers and playlists.
  3. Partner shortlist: brands ranked for the target age group, with the reason for each.
  4. Cities and timing: where the fans cluster and whether interest is rising.
  5. Cliché check: what a plain LLM suggested next to what the data says.

Why only Qloo: every number in the brief comes from the taste graph (audience affinity, demographics, trends). Our local A/B test showed plain Gemini guesses these and often contradicts the measured data.

User journey

Five steps, one screen each. The wait on step 3 is the show: the agent's work is visible.

How it works

The agent plans, calls Qloo in parallel, checks its own shortlist, then writes. Gemini never invents affinities; it only explains the data it was given.

Parallel modules

Audience, sound, partners, cities and timing run at once, so the slow calls do not add up.

Critic step

After the first pass, the agent ranks the partner shortlist for the target age group and drops weak or off-category picks.

Provenance

Every card keeps the endpoint, entity id and score it came from, shown on click.

Build size

The agent, tools and local UI already exist. New work: guided input, progress view, brief page, cliché-check logic, export.

Speed

Parallel calls plus cache keep the brief near 30 s. The progress screen turns waiting into the demo.

Cost control

Resolve ids once, trim tool output, cache per property. Fits the 50k calls per month.

Three-minute demo and how judges will read it

Demo script

  1. 0:00 "Adapting a cult novel? Ask a normal LLM for partners." Show the cliché answer.
  2. 0:20 Type The Secret History, pick the right entity, set target 25 to 34.
  3. 0:40 Watch the live checklist; each line names its Qloo call.
  4. 1:10 Brief opens: audience skew, mainstream and emerging artists, partners with reasons.
  5. 2:00 Cliché tab: the LLM's picks versus the data. Click a number to see its source.
  6. 2:40 Export the one-pager; change the title to a game and run it again.

Judging criteria

Qloo is the differentiator Every claim is a Qloo number with a visible source; the A/B tab shows the LLM alone is wrong or generic.

Agentic Plans modules, resolves ambiguity, runs tools in parallel, criticises and prunes its own shortlist.

Real value Replaces slow focus groups for a real buyer; the output is a document people act on.

Startup Per-brief or seat pricing for agencies and publishers; same engine serves creators finding sponsors (SponsorFit) as a second product.

Polish Disambiguation card, live progress, provenance on click, shareable page.
